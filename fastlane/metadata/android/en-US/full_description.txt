Puzzle Press is seven small puzzles, a new set every day: a word guess, a grouping game, a pangram hunt, a mini crossword, a word search, a letter-jump game, and sudoku.

Wordrow: guess a five-letter word in six tries.
Clusters: sort sixteen items into four groups of four.
Heptagram: find every word made from seven given letters, one of which must appear in each word.
Minigrid: a small daily crossword.
Wordweave: a themed word search.
Edgeways: jump from letter to letter to spell words.
Sudoku: classic sudoku at three difficulties.

Each puzzle changes once a day, and you can play a past day from the archive at any time. The app tracks your streak.

The app has no internet permission, so it never makes a network request. There's no account and no ads, and it doesn't track you. Your streak and progress stay in local storage on your phone.

Puzzle Press is free and open source software.
